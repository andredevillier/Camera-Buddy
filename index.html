Privacy Policy

Camera Buddy  ·  Last updated: September 2026

Camera Buddy is a personal assistant for DSLR and mirrorless photographers. This privacy policy describes how the app handles your information — which is simply to say that it does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information whatsoever.

Information We Collect

None. Camera Buddy does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, crash reports, or any other data from you or your device.

The app runs entirely on your device. It does not connect to any server, database, or third-party service. No account is required to use the app.

Data Storage

All information used by the app — your key selection, chord choices, rhythm patterns, and tuning preferences — exists only in your device's memory while the app is running and is not saved between sessions. Nothing is written to persistent storage, and nothing ever leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Camera Buddy does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services.

Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If the app is updated in a way that changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the date above will reflect the change. Any future data collection would be disclosed here before it takes effect.
